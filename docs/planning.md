# Project Proposal

## Business Case

### Problem statement
bristol has different areas with different health needs, but it can sometimes be difficult for residents to find and understand health statistics about the area they live in. although health information is available online, it may be spread across different sources and can be difficult for people to compare. i want to develop bristolpulse to make this information easier to access by allowing users to select an area of bristol and view important health statistics in one place

### Business benefits
the main benefits of bristolpulse includes:
makes local health information easier for bristol residents to access.
allows users to explore health statistics for different areas in bristol
helps users to compare their local areas health statistics with the bristols average.
presents informations in a simple and understandable way using visualisations
provides students and researchers with an easier way to explore local health data
helps raise awareness of health differences between communities.

### Options Considered
I considered a few different ways users could access local health information:

bristol open data website - users can search for and access different data sets themselves, but they may need some knowledge of how to find and understand the information.

Government and public health websites - these provides health statistics and reports, although users may need to search through different pages to find information about a specific bristol area.

Council reports -  Users can access published reports containing local health information, but these may not always present the data in a simple or interactive format.

I decided that BristolPulse would be a useful alternative because it aims to bring relevant local health statistics together in one place and make them easier to explore. 

### Expected Risks
There are some risks that could  affect the development and use of `bristolPulse':

Data Availability : Some health statistics may not be available for every area of bristol.
data accuracy - The information could be outdated or may not be uodated regularly.
technocal difficulties - i may experience difficuktues when connecting my application to thebristol open data API using java script 
time management - Developing the application alongside my university work may make it difficult to complete everything on time 
data Presentation -  If the statistics are not displayed clearly, users may misunderstand the information. 

I will try to reduce these risks by checking the available data sets, testing the application regularly and making sure the information is presented clearly. 

## Project Scope
The scope of BristolPulse is to develop a web-based application that allows users to explore health statistics for different areas of bristol 
the system will include :
allowing users to select an area of bristol 
accessing relevant health data sets from the bristol open data API
displaying health statistics such as life expectancy, smoking rates and long term health conditions, where suitable data is available 
allowing users to compare local health statistics with the bristol average, where comparable data is available 
presenting health information using charts and other visual elemants 
providing a simple and user-friendly interface that makes the information easy to understand 
The system will not provide medical advice, personal health assessments or individual medical records. it will focus on presenting publicly available health statistics for information and awarness purposes.
